# Product requirements by delivery stage

These documents turn the existing requirements into smaller reviewable scopes and acceptance scenarios. They identify the user-visible result, the evidence needed to accept it, and the implementation gaps. They do not replace the [requirements](../../Requirements%20Doc.md) or relax the [technical design](../../Technical%20Design.md).

| Delivery stage | Requirements breakdown | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| F1 Foundation | [Foundation](Foundation.md) | Establish trusted access, recoverable storage, ordered synchronization, durable work, and actionable health before app features depend on them. |

The F1–F10 delivery sequence remains defined in technical design §18. A breakdown document is a planning and review aid; it is not evidence that a stage is complete. Current runnable behavior belongs in the [CLI reference](../CLI/README.md) and [development instructions](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
